Shipping & delivery

  • Digital items download immediately after payment and are emailed to you.
  • Printed items in India are printed to order and posted within 2 working days; delivery is usually 4–7 days.
  • International printed items are printed in the destination country where we can, typically 5–10 working days.
  • Shipping is charged at checkout before you pay. Tracking is emailed when it ships.

Refunds & cancellations

  • Digital products: if it will not open or it is not what we described, tell us within 14 days and we refund in full.
  • Printed products: damaged or misprinted items are reprinted or refunded, no return needed. Photograph it and email us within 7 days.
  • Personalised books can be cancelled free within 60 minutes of ordering, before printing starts. After that we will fix any error we made, once, free.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Privacy

  • We collect your email, your order details, and for personalised books the child's first name, age and interests.
  • Photographs of children are used once, to draw the character, and are deleted from our systems within 30 days. They are never used in marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone except the printer, who only receives the finished artwork.
  • We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not track you across other websites.
  • Ask us to delete your data any time and we will, within 30 days.

Terms

  • The stories, characters and illustrations belong to us. Buying a book gives you the right to read, print and enjoy it at home — not to resell or redistribute it.
  • Digital files are for one family's use. Schools and nurseries, get in touch and we will sort out a licence.
  • Prices include GST where it applies. International orders may attract local duties.
