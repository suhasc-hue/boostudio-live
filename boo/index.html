Are the pictures reused between books?

No. Every book, and every page of the colouring book, is drawn for that book alone. That is the whole reason they take as long as they do.

How fast do I get the digital books?

Straight away once checkout is live. Right now, order over Instagram DM and a real person sends the file back the same day.

What happens to my child's photo?

It stays on your device until you place the order. We use it once to draw the character, delete it within 30 days, and never use it in marketing without written permission.

What if my child hates it?

Tell us and we refund you. Personalised books we will redo once, free.
