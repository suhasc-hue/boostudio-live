Who makes this

Boo's people

Boo started as a two-minute video and turned into a small girl who a lot of children now say goodnight to. She is curious, she is brave in a quiet way, and she is almost always slightly muddy.

Everything here is made by us: the stories, the pictures, the books. We make things we would happily give to our own children, and nothing we wouldn't.

Questions

Before you ask

How fast do I get the digital books?

Straight away. The download link is on the screen the second the payment clears, and it is emailed to you as well.

Can I print them at home?

Yes. Everything is A4 and made to print on a normal home printer. The colouring books deliberately use less ink.

Do you post real books?

Yes, printed and posted from within India, usually 4 to 7 days. International orders print in the country they are going to.

What happens to my child's photo?

It is used once, to draw the character, and then deleted. We never put a customer's child in our marketing without asking you first, in writing.

What if my child hates it?

Tell us and we will refund you. For personalised books we will redo it once, free, if something is wrong.
