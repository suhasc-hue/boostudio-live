10% goes to educating a girl child

Because of you, someone else gets to learn to read too.

The rule

10% of every sale on this site — calculated on net revenue after payment fees and taxes — is set aside for the education of girls, and paid out every month.

Where it goes

Recipient NGO being finalised — first transfer and receipt will be published on the giving page.

The receipts

Every transfer is published here: the amount, the date, the recipient and the receipt number. If this page is empty, nothing has been collected yet — and we would rather show you an empty page than a number we cannot prove.
